LESSON TRACKER - FILES IN THIS FOLDER
=====================================

Upload ALL of these files together (not the folder itself).

index.html            The app itself.
sw.js                 Lets the app open without internet after the first visit.
manifest.webmanifest  App name and icons used when added to the home screen.

HOME-SCREEN ICONS (all three show the same picture at different sizes)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
File           Size (pixels)   Format   Used for
icon-180.png   180 x 180       PNG      iPad / iPhone home-screen icon
icon-192.png   192 x 192       PNG      Browser tab icon, Android home screen
icon-512.png   512 x 512       PNG      Large icon (install screen, splash)

To use your own icon, make three images with exactly these
file names, sizes and format, then replace these files:
  - Square, with a solid background (no transparency -
    iPads fill transparent areas with black).
  - Don't round the corners - the iPad does that itself.
  - Keep the important part within the centre 80%
    (the edges may be trimmed on some devices).

LOGOS UPLOADED IN THE APP (Settings > Logos & signature)
--------------------------------------------------------
These are NOT files in this folder - they are saved on the device.
Any PNG or JPG works; a PNG with a transparent background looks best.
  App logo                about 300 x 300 px   shown at the top of the app
  Invoice & receipt logo  about 600 px wide    printed on invoices and receipts
  Signature               about 600 x 200 px   printed on receipts
Larger images are shrunk automatically.
